macOS — First launch setup
  • 1
    Open the .dmg and drag SwiftShareX to Applications.
  • 2
    Launch it — macOS will block it. Click Done.
  • 3
    Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  • 4
    Scroll down and click Open Anyway next to SwiftShareX.
  • 5
    Confirm with Open. You're all set!
